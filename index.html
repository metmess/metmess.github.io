About

The 6th edition comes to Kharagpur

The Department of Geology & Geophysics, IIT Kharagpur, in association with the Physical Research Laboratory, Ahmedabad, is organising MetMeSS 2026 — a planetary science symposium on the origin and evolution of the Solar System.

The programme spans state-of-the-art laboratory analyses of Earth and planetary materials alongside modelling work that deciphers the evolution of planetary and celestial bodies and the processes that shape them. Two days of talks, posters and discussion, bracketed by a two-day pre-conference school for students and a two-day post-conference field trip.

Scientific programme

Themes & sessions

Abstracts are invited across the following sessions. Indicate your preferred session at submission.

1

Early solar system processes & organics

Presolar grains, IDPs, chronology and the first solids of the Solar System.

2

Differentiated bodies

Asteroids, the Moon and Mars — differentiation, magmatism and planetary evolution.

3

Asteroids, comets, micrometeorites & returned samples

Small-body science and material returned by sample-return missions.

4

Planetary analogues

Terrestrial analogue sites and what they teach us about planetary processes.

5

Planetary remote sensing & instrumentation

Orbital and in-situ observation, payload design and data analysis.

6

Space weathering, meteors & the atmosphere

Surface processing of airless bodies; the role of meteors in atmospheric and ionospheric processes.

Registration & abstracts

Registration and abstract submission are handled in a single online form. You will receive a registration ID by email immediately on submission — quote it in all correspondence.

How it works

  1. Fill the registration form — your details, academic status and (optionally) your abstract with all co-authors.
  2. Pay the fee by UPI. The form shows the exact amount and our QR code, then asks for your UPI transaction reference.
  3. Receive your registration ID (format KGP2000) by email, along with the abstract template.
  4. Await confirmation — we verify the payment and confirm within three working days.
Register & submit abstract Abstract template (DOCX)

Abstracts must use the official template — one page, Times New Roman 10 pt, US Letter. Abstracts in any other format will be returned.

Getting there

Venue, travel & stay

IIT Kharagpur

Founded in 1951 as the first of the Indian Institutes of Technology, IIT Kharagpur hosts the Department of Geology & Geophysics — among the oldest earth science departments in the country. The symposium will be held on the main campus in Kharagpur, West Bengal.

Reaching Kharagpur

  • By air — Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport, Kolkata (~130 km), then road or rail.
  • By rail — Kharagpur Junction is a major stop on the Howrah–Chennai line; ~2 hours from Howrah.
  • By road — NH-16 connects Kharagpur to Kolkata and Bhubaneswar.

Ground rules

Code of conduct

MetMeSS is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free environment for every participant, regardless of background, career stage or identity.

Integrity first

Honesty and ethical conduct in research and in scientific discourse are non-negotiable.

Respect for difference

Treat every participant with professionalism and respect. Harassment, intimidation and abuse of power will not be tolerated.

Strive for excellence

We hold the highest standards of scientific inquiry and open knowledge-sharing.

Concerns during the symposium should be raised with any member of the organising committee, or by email to the convenors. Reports will be handled confidentially.
