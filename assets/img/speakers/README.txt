Format: square JPG, 600x600 px or larger, under 300 KB.
Faces are cropped to a circle, so leave a little headroom.

Ask each speaker to send a photo when they accept the invitation — one line
in the acceptance email is enough:

  "If you are happy for us to include your photograph on the symposium
   website, please attach a square headshot in your reply."

That gives you permission in writing along with the file.
